Week of November 8, 2026 · Information as of this date

Where things stand this week

Late November looks like a busy stretch in Japan. Based on information in Japan, a new Pokémon expansion is set for November 27 and a new ONE PIECE booster for November 21. Dragon Ball Fusion World's next booster is reportedly listed for December 12.

Pokémon Card Game

Official

  • Expansion Pack "Hadou Seeker" (M7): November 27, 2026 / ¥200 per pack (tax included), 5 cards per pack
  • Special Deck Set MEGA (Mega Feraligatr, Mega Dragonite, Mega Gengar): November 13, 2026 / ¥5,500

Reported

  • "Hadou Seeker" is reported to feature Mega Evolution ex cards with a "Z" in their names, such as Mega Lucario Z ex, Mega Absol Z ex and Mega Garchomp Z ex.

Still unclear

  • Japanese media report that no products after December had been officially announced as of late October, including the high-class pack that has often come out around this time of year.

Japan vs overseas

  • No overseas release date is confirmed in this report.

What changed: "Hadou Seeker" was long known only as a trademarked name, and its release date circulated as a rumor. Reports say the official site announced it on October 23, 2026, which appears to have moved it from rumor to confirmed.
