Weekly TCG Report from Japan

Week of November 8, 2026 · Information as of this date

Where things stand this week

Late November looks like a busy stretch in Japan. A new Pokémon expansion, "Hadou Seeker", is set for November 27 and ONE PIECE OP-18 for November 21, and both are being sold through store lotteries in Japan. Dragon Ball Fusion World's next booster, "REACH THE GOD", now has an official date of December 12.

Official on an official page (as reported)   Reported by Japanese media or shops   Still unclear not yet known   Japan vs overseas how Japan and overseas differ

Pokémon Card Game

Official

  • Expansion Pack "Hadou Seeker" (M7): November 27, 2026 / ¥200 per pack (tax included), 5 cards per pack
  • Cards shown on the official page include Mega Lucario Z ex, Mega Absol Z ex, Mega Garchomp Z ex, Floatzel ex and Sigilyph ex, listed at RR rarity.
  • Special Deck Set MEGA (Mega Feraligatr, Mega Dragonite, Mega Gengar): November 13, 2026 / ¥5,500

Reported

  • Mega Lucario Z ex is the card most watched by Japanese collectors, as the set's featured Mega Evolution. Japanese card media expect higher-rarity versions (SAR, MUR) based on past MEGA sets, but none had been officially shown as of November 6.
  • Pre-orders in Japan are mostly by lottery. One Japanese tracker counted 98 active store lotteries on November 6, up from 8 on October 24.
  • Pre-order box listings on Japanese shopping sites start around ¥11,000–13,000 (early November, as reported by Japanese card media). The number of packs per box has not been officially announced.

Still unclear

  • The full card list and box configuration
  • Products after December: Japanese media report none had been officially announced as of late October, including the high-class pack that has often come out around this time of year.

Japan vs overseas

  • No English version of "Hadou Seeker" has been announced. "Aura Seeker" is a name used by overseas fans and shops, not an official English title.

What changed: "Hadou Seeker" was long known only as a trademarked name, and its release date circulated as a rumor. Reports say the official site announced it on October 23, 2026, which appears to have moved it from rumor to confirmed. Since then, store lotteries in Japan have grown quickly.

ONE PIECE Card Game

Official

  • OP-18 "The Dominance of God": November 21, 2026 / ¥240 per pack, 6 cards / 127 + 1 card types

Reported

  • 24 packs per box (¥5,760 per box, tax included)
  • Theme: the Water Seven arc and the Holy Knights of God, with six new leaders reported, including Luffy, Franky, Spandam and Sengoku.
  • A new "Holy Knights of God Super Parallel" rarity is reported. Japanese card media expect it to be the set's top chase card, comparing it with the high-priced Super Parallels of earlier sets.
  • Reported SP cards include Nefertari Vivi, Law & Bepo, Edward Newgate and Charlotte Linlin, along with a Comic Parallel Robin.
  • Store lotteries in Japan are closing this week. For example, Premium Bandai's lottery is listed as closing on November 12.

Still unclear

  • The official rarity breakdown and pull rates

Japan vs overseas

  • The English version page reportedly lists November 20, 2026, one day before the Japanese release on November 21.

What changed: earlier dates seen in Japan (11/22 and 11/28) appear to have been replaced by November 21 once the official product page went up. Card reveals have continued, and Japanese media now list most of the set.

Dragon Ball Super Card Game Fusion World

Official

  • Booster Pack "REACH THE GOD" [FB12]: December 12, 2026 / ¥240 per pack (tax included), 6 cards per pack / 123 card types
  • The official Japanese account says the lineup centers on characters from "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus", the anime set to air from this fall.

Reported

  • The previous booster, "BRIGHTNESS OF HOPE" [FB11], came out on September 12, 2026, and was reported to include a collaboration with Dragon Ball Legends.

Still unclear

  • Rarity breakdown and most of the card list for FB12

Japan vs overseas

  • An overseas shop reports that the official English page lists December 18, 2026, six days after the Japanese release.

What changed: last week FB12 was known only from a Japanese release calendar. The official product page now lists the date, price and card count.
