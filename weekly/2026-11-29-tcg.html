Weekly TCG Report from Japan

Week of November 29, 2026 · Information as of November 28, 2026 (JST)

Where things stand this week

Pokémon Center Online is running a lottery for a 30th-anniversary Pokémon figure that comes with one random card. Entries close on November 30, and the official page says delivery is limited to addresses in Japan. Ichiban Kuji Dragon Ball "Fantastic Adventure 3", a prize lottery, started at stores in Japan on November 28. Pokémon "Hadou Seeker" (M7) went on sale in Japan on November 27.

Currency: prices are in Japanese yen (¥), because that is what Japanese shops show. Dollar figures are rough conversions at about ¥159.3 = US$1 (Bank of Japan central rate, November 27, 2026; rates move daily, and your bank or card may use a different rate). We have not shown pound figures this week. Quick guide: ¥1,000 ≈ $6.3 · ¥5,000 ≈ $31 · ¥10,000 ≈ $63. Figures do not include shipping, proxy fees or import charges.

Only in Japan this week

Pokémon Card Game "Card Illustration Figure Collection 30th CELEBRATION"

  • Official A Pikachu figure based on a card illustration, a stand with a display frame, and one card. The card is picked at random from 13 types listed on the product page (12 Pikachu cards from the "30th CELEBRATION" expansion plus one secret card). ¥1,800 including tax (about $11), one per person.
  • Official Sold by lottery on Pokémon Center Online. Entries: November 27, 16:00 to November 30, 16:59 (JST). Results from December 4, 15:00. Winners order between December 4 and December 8. Shipping is planned for February 1–15, 2027.
  • Official There are two entry tracks. Pokémon Center Online says most winning slots go to members who verified their identity on the Pokémon Card Game Players Club, which uses Japan's My Number Card. Members without verification can still enter, but the notice says verification makes winning easier.
  • Japan vs overseas The product page says delivery is limited to addresses in Japan. The page does not mention a release outside Japan.

Ichiban Kuji Dragon Ball "Fantastic Adventure 3"

  • Official Scheduled to start at stores in Japan from November 28, in order of store arrival. One draw costs ¥800 including tax (about $5). The online store lists a scheduled start of December 1 at 17:00 (JST). The official page says dates and details are subject to change.
  • Japan vs overseas Unknown (no official announcement). The product page does not mention overseas sales. The official "Overseas Information" page lists regional sites and tells readers to check each region's official social media, but it does not mention this product.

Pokémon Card Game

Official

  • MEGA expansion "Hadou Seeker" (M7): on sale in Japan from November 27, 2026. ¥200 per pack including tax (about $1.30), 5 cards per pack.
  • It includes "Makishi no Nekketsu", a Special Art Rare of Crasher Wake (Makishi in Japanese) drawn by manga artist Tetsuo Hara.

Still unclear

  • How the set can be bought in Japan now that the earlier Pokémon Center Online lottery has closed. The official release page does not describe lottery, reservation or purchase methods.

Japan vs overseas

  • "Hadou Seeker" has no official English name yet, so we use the Japanese name.

Official

  • A new "banned pair" took effect on November 27, 2026: EB05-001 Jewelry Bonney cannot be in the same deck as OP13-001 Monkey.D.Luffy or OP01-002 Trafalgar Law (parallel cards included). The official English site posted the same notice.

Other releases

  • UNION ARENA "D.Gray-man" (UA58BT): on sale in Japan from November 27. The official page lists new art by original creator Katsura Hoshino and limited serial-numbered AP cards. We did not find an English-edition date on the official site.
