Christmas 2026: getting Japanese cards in time

If you want Japanese cards under the tree, the question is not only what to buy but when the parcel has to leave Japan. This section works backwards from December 25, shows what an order really costs, and lists the country rules that changed this year.

The short version
  • Our suggestion Treat about December 5 as the last comfortable date to have your parcel leave Japan by regular airmail to France or the UK, and about December 12 by EMS. These dates include a one-week safety margin (see the table).
  • Our suggestion Your purchase has to happen earlier than that. The item must first reach the proxy's warehouse, so plan to buy about a week before those dates. Aim to finish buying by around the end of November.
  • Our suggestion Combine items only if they will all be at the warehouse within a few days of each other. A combined parcel leaves only when the last item arrives, so do not hold back items that are already there for a slow one.
  • Our suggestion Cards that are already in stock in Japan (singles, sets released earlier) are safer for Christmas than new sets sold by lottery.
  • Official Germany: Japan Post no longer accepts online-sales mail to Germany (from November 9), so use a courier option. EU: new charges apply this year. US: advance electronic declaration is required for most mail.

Why the purchase date matters more than the shipping date

With a proxy service such as Buyee, a purchase goes through three stages, and the table above only covers the last one:

  1. Seller to warehouse (inside Japan): the Japanese seller ships the item to the proxy's warehouse. How fast this happens depends on the seller. Individual sellers on marketplaces can take days to ship.
  2. At the warehouse: the item is received and checked, and you pay for international shipping. If you combine several purchases into one parcel, it waits for the last item.
  3. Japan to your country: the part covered by the table. Customs checks can add days.

Our suggestion For a gift you need by December 25, we suggest finishing your purchases about a week before the "leave Japan by" date for your country and shipping each parcel as soon as it is ready. Step-by-step details are in our Buyee guide.

How to buy so it arrives in time

  • Our suggestion Choose items that are already available in Japan. Single cards and sets released earlier can be bought and shipped at once. For new sets, Reported some pre-orders in Japan are sold by store lottery, and lottery entries and pickup can take weeks.
  • Our suggestion Combine or split depending on timing. If everything is already at the warehouse, or will be within a few days, combine it into one parcel to save on shipping. If one item is a pre-order or comes from a slow seller, ship the ready items on their own and accept the extra shipping cost, so that nothing waits for the slowest item.
  • Our suggestion Use a tracked courier for the last-minute order. Buyee says courier and Air Delivery parcels ship the next business day after you pay.
  • Japanese cards are printed in Japanese. Make sure the person receiving them wants Japanese versions. Sets and card lists can differ from the English versions.
  • For single cards, check the photos and the condition description carefully. Labels such as "graded" or "authenticated" are set by sellers or third parties, and proxy services generally do not guarantee authenticity.

Country notes

Germany

  • Official In a notice dated November 4, 2026, Japan Post revised its International Mail Conditions Table, effective November 9. Because of EU tax reform, B2C (online sales) mail to Germany can no longer be sent.
  • Official Buyee suspended international mail to Germany on November 10, 2026. This covers EMS, International Parcel (AIR and Sea), Small Packet (AIR) and AIR Packet. Buyee recommends Buyee Air Delivery for packages to Germany.

France and the rest of the EU

  • Official Since July 1, 2026, goods worth €150 or less are no longer duty-free in the EU. A flat €3 customs duty applies per item, according to a law-firm briefing and Japan Post.
  • Official From November 1, 2026, Japan Post says a €2 Union Handling Fee also applies to online-sales parcels.
  • Still unclear We could not confirm how a parcel with many different cards is counted for the €3 duty, or how VAT is shown for each shipping method. Check the breakdown at checkout.
  • Official Japan Post's conditions table also changed on November 9 for several other EU countries, including Italy, Spain and the Netherlands (rules on IOSS, DDP/DAP and product codes). If you live in one of them, check at Buyee's checkout which shipping methods are offered and how import charges are shown before you pay.

United States

  • Official Japan Post accepts mail to the US again (since October 15, 2026). Most mail items need an advance electronic declaration through a provider such as Zonos or SafePackage, with a "DDP" label. Provider fees apply even if no duty is due.
  • Official Game cards and similar items must be sent as Small Packet or EMS (Goods), not as printed matter. Japan Post's notice does not mention trading cards by name, so confirm with your proxy service.
  • Official Buyee changed how it calculates and bills prepaid duties and customs clearance fees for US shipments, effective April 21, 2026. Check the total at checkout.

Other destinations

  • Official Some routes are suspended. As of November 12, 2026, Buyee listed Japan Post EMS and airmail to Qatar and Kuwait as suspended. Check Buyee's notices page for your country before you buy.
