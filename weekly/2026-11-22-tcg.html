Weekly TCG Report from Japan

Week of November 22, 2026 · Information as of November 20, 2026

Where things stand this week

Two Japanese sets went on sale on Saturday, November 21: ONE PIECE "The Dominance of God" (OP-18) and Yu-Gi-Oh! OCG REVOLUTION BOOSTER RV02. The Pokémon expansion "Hadou Seeker" follows on Friday, November 27. Its Pokémon Center Online lottery has already closed, so buyers in Japan who missed it will be looking at store stock on release day. Separately, the special Yu-Gi-Oh! pack offered through the "Yu-Gi-Oh! Jump" magazine cannot be ordered from overseas.

Currency: prices are in Japanese yen (¥), because that is what Japanese shops and Buyee show. Dollar figures are rough conversions at about ¥159.6 = US$1 (Bank of Japan central rate, November 18, 2026; rates move daily, and your bank or card may use a different rate). We have not shown pound figures this week. Quick guide: ¥1,000 ≈ $6.3 · ¥5,000 ≈ $31 · ¥10,000 ≈ $63. Figures do not include shipping, proxy fees or import charges.

Only in Japan this week

  • Official "Yu-Gi-Oh! Jump" mail-in pack. The 30th-anniversary special issue "遊☆戯☆王ジャンプ" (Yu-Gi-Oh! Jump) went on sale on November 13 at ¥990 (about $6.20). Buyers can order the OCG pack "LEGENDARY DUEL SELECTION" for ¥700 (about $4.40, shipping included). Orders run until December 23, 2026, 23:58 Japan time. A serial code is needed: it is printed inside the first-edition print copy, or comes with the Kindle or Zebrack digital edition (one code per account, available until December 14, 16:59).
  • Official The order page states that orders from overseas are not accepted and that it does not ship outside Japan. Packs ship from early March 2027, with delivery completing by late April 2027.
  • Reported There is information in Japan that the pack contains a new card, "極限を超えし者-バスター・ブレイダー" (a new Buster Blader card, Prismatic Secret Rare), plus "De-Fusion" and "Enemy Controller" (Secret Rare).
  • Japan vs overseas Readers abroad cannot order this pack directly. Whether a given proxy service can handle a mail-in order like this depends on each service's own rules.
  • Official Ichiban Kuji Dragon Ball "Fantastic Adventure 3", a Japan-only prize lottery, starts on November 28 at convenience stores such as FamilyMart and at hobby shops. Each draw costs ¥800 (about $5).

ONE PIECE Card Game

Official

  • "The Dominance of God" (OP-18): Japan on November 21, 2026, at ¥240 per pack (about $1.50), 6 cards per pack.
  • The English edition is listed for November 20, 2026, at $4.99 per pack.
  • A Japanese box holds 24 packs, as printed on the box. At ¥240 a pack, that is ¥5,760 (about $36).

Reported

  • There is information in Japan that the leaders include Franky, and that the themes are the Water Seven arc, the Holy Knights of God, and Elbaf and the World Government.
  • There is also information in Japan that the official pages list three special cards: a Saint Shamrock alt art illustrated by Yoshitaka Amano (OP18-119), a Nico Robin Super Alt Art (OP18-031), and a 4th-anniversary Holy Knights alt art of Saint Gunko (OP18-065). Japanese media call the Saint Gunko card a "Super Parallel."

Still unclear

  • How single-card prices settle in the first week. We will cover first-week prices in next week's report.

Pokémon Card Game

Official

  • MEGA expansion "Hadou Seeker" (M7): Japan on November 27, 2026, at ¥200 per pack (about $1.25), 5 cards per pack.

  • Correction: last week we listed parallel versions of Loki and Shamrock illustrated by Yoshitaka Amano among the OP-18 cards. The Amano Loki card (OP17-119) belongs to OP-17, not OP-18. In OP-18, the Amano card is Saint Shamrock (OP18-119).
