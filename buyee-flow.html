How Overseas Buyers Shop with Buyee

Purchase → Japanese warehouse → international delivery. With Buyee you usually pay twice: once for the item and once for international shipping.

Purchase Payment Shipping
① 1st paymentAt purchase: item price + fees
② Warehouse storage30 days free, up to 90 days
③ 2nd paymentAfter arrival: international shipping, etc.
  1. 1

    Sign up Purchase

    Create a free Buyee account. The site is available in English, Chinese and other languages, so you don't need to read Japanese.

    Done by you

  2. 2

    Find items Purchase

    • Search within Buyee: JDirectItems Auction / Fleamarket / Shopping, Mercari, Rakuten, Rakuma, Amazon and more
    • Buy directly from the “Buyee cart” on partner shops' official sites
    • Add items from Japanese sites with the browser extension or app
    • Paste the URL of any item to request a purchase (URL purchase)
  3. 3

    Bid or order = 1st payment Payment

    Place a bid for auctions, or a purchase request for online shops and flea markets. At this point you pay the item price (plus domestic shipping, Buyee fees, etc.). For auctions, the amount is finalized when you win.

    Payment methods: PayPal, credit cards (VISA / Mastercard / JCB), Alipay, UnionPay

    You → Buyee (Buyee pays the Japanese seller or shop on your behalf)

  4. 4

    Seller ships to Buyee's warehouse in Japan Shipping

    Japanese sellers and shops ship to Buyee's domestic warehouse in Japan, not overseas, so it's handled as a domestic sale.

    For flea markets and auctions, the time until shipping varies by seller.
  5. 5

    Arrival, storage & packing options Shipping

    You'll be notified by email and on My Page when the item arrives. Check the weighed package details under “Package Info”.

    • Package consolidation: combine several items into one box to save on international shipping
    • Order options such as protective packing from My Page
    • Storage is free for 30 days after arrival, with daily fees from day 31, up to 90 days
    If international shipping isn't paid within 90 days, items may be disposed of.
  6. 6

    Choose shipping = 2nd payment Payment

    The price is fixed by the weight and size after packing. You pay international shipping (plus option fees and, in eligible countries, prepaid duties).

    Main shipping methods: Japan Post, FedEx, DHL, UPS, Buyee Air Delivery, ECMS, etc. (options vary by country/region)

  7. 7

    Shipped overseas → delivered Shipping

    Japan Post / FedEx / DHL / UPS / Buyee Air DeliveryShips the business day after payment
    Buyee Air Delivery (Taiwan) / Domestic delivery2–3 business days after payment
    ECMSShips every Mon, Wed and Fri
    Duties and import taxes not collected in advance may be charged on delivery (depends on country and carrier).
