About Us

Our purpose

JapanMania is an information site that compares the prices of trading cards listed on Japanese auctions and flea markets with overseas market prices, and explains how collectors abroad can buy them from Japan through proxy services. Our goal is to help people who can't easily reach Japanese listings because of language or unfamiliar systems search with confidence and the information they need.

How We Compare Prices

  • Overseas average: based on recent completed sales of the same card outside Japan. Graded and ungraded copies are compared separately where possible.
  • Japan price: the current bid or Buy It Now price on the Japanese listing, converted to USD at the rate on the day we checked. Auction prices usually rise before the auction ends.
  • Fees and shipping: not included in the prices above. Proxy service fees, international shipping and import duties depend on your country and shipping method, so always check the final amount at checkout.

Editorial policy

  • We always state the date we checked any price we publish.
  • Labels such as “Graded” or “Authenticated” come from sellers or third-party systems. This site does not guarantee authenticity or condition.
  • We do not take instructions from advertisers on our content or evaluations.
  • If we find an error or receive a correction, we check it and fix it promptly.

How we earn money

This site participates in affiliate programs of proxy services such as Buyee. If a purchase is made through a link on this site, we may receive a referral commission, but the amount you pay does not change.

What we don't do

We do not sell, inspect or ship products, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by Buyee or any marketplace. For questions about orders, payment or delivery, please contact the proxy service you used.
