How to Buy Pokémon Cards from Japan

Japanese auctions and flea markets list many Pokémon cards that are hard to find outside Japan. This page covers Japanese keywords that help when searching on Buyee from abroad, and what to check in a listing.

Why search in Japan

Japanese-edition cards
Japanese-edition expansion packs and promo cards handed out at events and campaigns in Japan are examples of cards that mainly circulate in Japan.
Many listings
Japanese auctions and flea markets list everything from single cards to sealed boxes and bulk lots, giving you more options.
Know the total cost
The listing price is only part of what you pay. Before you buy, add up Buyee fees, international shipping and import duties.

Japanese search keywords for Buyee

Buyee can be searched in English, but searching with Japanese keywords also finds listings that sellers wrote only in Japanese.

KeywordMeaning / how to use
ポケモンカード ／ ポケカOfficial name and short form for Pokémon cards. Search both to miss fewer listings.
未開封Unopened. Use it when looking for boxes or packs.
シュリンク付きThe box still has its outer plastic wrap (shrink wrap).
美品“Good condition”, but it is the seller's own judgment.
PSA10 ／ 鑑定品Cards graded by a grading company. Always check the certification number.
プロモPromo cards handed out at events or campaigns.
旧裏Early Japanese-edition cards (up to around 2001) with a different card-back design.
SAR ／ AR ／ SR ／ URRarity labels. Combine them with the card name when searching.
まとめ売りSeveral cards sold together as a lot.

Checkpoints when viewing a listing

  • Check the photos for scratches and whitening on the front, back, corners and edges
  • Check whether it is the Japanese or English edition (prices can differ for the same card name)
  • “Graded” is the seller's own claim. Verify the certification number on the grading company's website
  • Be careful with listings far below market price or with few photos
  • Check the seller's ratings and track record
  • Before bidding, estimate the total including Buyee fees, international shipping and import duties
